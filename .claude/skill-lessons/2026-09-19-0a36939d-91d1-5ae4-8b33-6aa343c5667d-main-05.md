# .claude/skills/jenkins-builds/SKILL.md — an unschedulable job is not a blocked task; check its sibling jobs first

- Added: 2026-09-19
- Applies to: .claude/skills/jenkins-builds/SKILL.md
- Evidence: a cleanup job sat queued for days on "There are no nodes with the label '<label>'" and was reported as impossible without Job/Configure, but two sibling jobs sharing its name prefix were healthy and one had a documented force mode for exactly that case; the work then took four ordinary builds.
- Proposed change: before reporting a job as unrunnable, enumerate sibling jobs by name prefix and check their recent build results and durations for one that performs the same action.
