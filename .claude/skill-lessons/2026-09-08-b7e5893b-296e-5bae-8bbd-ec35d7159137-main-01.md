# .claude/skills/linode-docker-provisioning/SKILL.md — a run.sh call that sleeps for minutes on a busy box dies with "Empty reply from server"

- Added: 2026-09-08
- Applies to: .claude/skills/linode-docker-provisioning/SKILL.md
- Evidence: While a detached setup ran under deliberate CPU saturation (load average ~15 on 6 vCPUs), two `run.sh <run_id> -- "sleep 480; ..."` and `"sleep 540; ..."` polls both failed with `curl: (52) Empty reply from server` — well under the documented 600s exec-server cap — while a `sleep 240` poll on the same box returned normally.
- Proposed change: In the polling guidance, cap an in-command `sleep` at roughly 240s rather than treating the 600s exec-server timeout as the usable budget, and note that "Empty reply from server" on a loaded box means the poll was too long, not that the VM or the detached job died.
