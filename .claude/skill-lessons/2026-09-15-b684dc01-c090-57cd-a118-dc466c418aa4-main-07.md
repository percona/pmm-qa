# .claude/agents/investigator.md — a reviewer's "Commit suggestion" moves the head without your push, and silently falsifies the PR body

- Added: 2026-09-15
- Applies to: .claude/agents/investigator.md
- Evidence: A reviewer applied an empty ```suggestion``` through GitHub's Commit suggestion button; the branch tip moved from the session's commit to a new one that deleted part of the fix, the review threads came back as resolved AND outdated rather than as a request, and the PR body went on claiming a change that no longer shipped until the head was fetched and diffed.
- Proposed change: In the review-event handling paragraph, add that every wake starts by fetching the PR head and comparing it with the local branch tip, and that a head the session did not push is re-diffed against the base and the body corrected in that same round — the live-document rule applies to a reviewer's commit, not only to your own push.
