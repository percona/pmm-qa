# .claude/agents/investigator.md — watch the upstream job, not the artifact, when waiting for a publish-style event

- Added: 2026-09-15
- Applies to: .claude/agents/investigator.md
- Evidence: Waiting to catch a ~3.5 min repository-inconsistency window, the session polled `repo.percona.com` every 120 s for seven consecutive turns with no signal. One `get_running_builds` call on the Jenkins `pmm` master then showed nothing running at all — and since `pmm3-client-repo-push` only fires after `pmm3-client-autobuild` (~50 min), no publish could occur for the next several minutes, so every one of those ticks was knowably wasted.
- Proposed change: When waiting for a short event produced by a known CI job, poll the job scheduler for a running predecessor instead of polling the artifact: a long-running upstream build is advance warning, its absence rules the event out for that lead time, and the watch can hold a cheap interval until a build appears and only then tighten.
