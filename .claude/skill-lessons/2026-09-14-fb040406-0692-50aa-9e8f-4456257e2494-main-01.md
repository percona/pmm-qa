- Added: 2026-09-14
- Applies to: .claude/skills/qa-code-review/SKILL.md
- Evidence: Check 7's second limit says "on a flake fix the explicit const is what carries the measured floor". The human reviewer took a third option and deleted the const, inlining `30` at both call sites (https://github.com/percona/pmm-qa/pull/1419#discussion_r4003571090, https://github.com/percona/pmm-qa/pull/1419#discussion_r4003572680, https://github.com/percona/pmm-qa/pull/1419#discussion_r4003574799); that is what merged, at `codeceptjs-e2e/tests/pages/dashboardPage.js:1200,1210`.
- Proposed change: Qualify check 7's second limit — what carries a measured floor is the explicit second argument at the call site, not a constant naming it; where the value has no caller outside the file, offer the inline literal as the third option instead of citing the flake fix as a reason the const must exist.
