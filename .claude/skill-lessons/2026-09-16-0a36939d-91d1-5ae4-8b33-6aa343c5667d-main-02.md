# .claude/agents/investigator.md — a publisher job finishing is not when the change goes live; do not read "unchanged" from one early probe

- Added: 2026-09-16
- Applies to: .claude/agents/investigator.md
- Evidence: Watching for a repository-inconsistency window, the session probed `repo.percona.com` at 10:29:30 and 10:31:38 — after Jenkins `pmm3-client-repo-push` #1568 had already reported SUCCESS at 10:28:44 — saw the old payload both times, and concluded and reported that the push had republished identical bytes and opened no window. By 10:48:30 the server was serving a different file (180478124 -> 180478156) whose `Last-Modified` was 10:26:18, i.e. the staging mtime preserved by `rsync -a` from during that same push. The change was real; it simply had not reached the serving host yet, and the conclusion drawn from the early probes was false.
- Proposed change: Warn that a publish pipeline's job completion marks staging, not visibility: artifact `Last-Modified` is the staging mtime (rsync -a preserves it), so it cannot be compared against job end times to decide whether anything changed, and a probe taken right after the job can still return the previous content. Never conclude "nothing changed" from probes bounded by the job's runtime — keep probing until the observed content changes or a stated deadline passes, and say the measurement is inconclusive rather than asserting a no-op.
