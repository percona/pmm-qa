# .claude/agents/investigator.md — a republish of identical bytes is not an event; count content changes, not job runs

- Added: 2026-09-16
- Applies to: .claude/agents/investigator.md
- Evidence: Hunting a repository-inconsistency window, the session treated each `pmm3-client-repo-push` run as an opportunity and counted "6 publishes/day" as six chances. Two full pushes were then watched end to end at 12-second resolution (#1567 aarch64 10:19:11-10:22:23, #1568 x86_64 10:25:17-10:28:44) with zero inconsistency, because both rebuilt the same commit (UPLOAD_PATH `.../v3/3b2879b0d/...`) and produced byte-identical packages: the pool file's Last-Modified never moved off 06:49:39, so rsync rewrote nothing and no window could exist.
- Proposed change: When waiting on a publish-triggered failure, treat the artifact's own change signal (Last-Modified/ETag, or the size/hash in the index) as the event, not the publisher job running, and check the build's commit or changeset before staking a measurement window on it — a rebuild of an unchanged commit republishes identical bytes and is a no-op for anything downstream.
