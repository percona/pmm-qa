# .claude/skills/codeceptjs-migration/mappings.md — Translate a locate(...).find('span') label locator to the wrapper testid, not to first()

- Added: 2026-08-24
- Evidence: Four row-4 source locators scope a testid down to a `span`. Under MUI that is multi-match, so a literal translation would be a strict-mode violation on `toContainText`/`toBeVisible`. The writer asserted on the single-element wrapper testid instead, whose innerText is the union of those spans, with no `first()` and no invented class selector. The initial gate confirmed all four live, and separately proved the hazard is real: the same page's advisors toggle matches 4 elements under the literal source selector.
- Proposed change: In `mappings.md`, add the mapping: a CodeceptJS `locate('$testid').find('<tag>')` label locator becomes `getByTestId('testid')` asserted with `toContainText`, not a `.locator('<tag>')` chain plus `first()`; narrow to a child testid only when MCP shows the wrapper itself is multi-match.
