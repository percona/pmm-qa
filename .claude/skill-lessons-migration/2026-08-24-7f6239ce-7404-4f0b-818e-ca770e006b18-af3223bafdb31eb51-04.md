# .claude/skills/codeceptjs-migration/audit-checklist.md — Prove title fidelity by extraction, not by eye

- Added: 2026-08-24
- Evidence: The "scenario titles preserved exactly" invariant was checked at the final gate with one scripted regex extraction printing the source and target titles as JSON string literals side by side. That form makes two things self-evident that reading cannot: a trailing-space or byte-level difference becomes visible, and the commented-out scenario appears in the source list so its absence from the target reads as correct rather than as something to argue about. It also confirmed the generated data-row suffix matches CodeceptJS's own `<title> | <JSON.stringify(row)>` construction, which was separately derived from the CodeceptJS data-table source rather than assumed.
- Proposed change: In `audit-checklist.md`, require the title-fidelity check to be done by extracting both title lists programmatically and comparing them as quoted strings, rather than by reading the two files; the extraction must include commented-out and skipped scenarios so their handling is visible in the same output.
