# .claude/skills/codeceptjs-migration/audit-checklist.md — neither gate checks idiom against the repo's dominant pattern, so idiom defects escape to the PR

- Added: 2026-09-04
- Applies to: `.claude/skills/codeceptjs-migration/audit-checklist.md`
- Evidence: Row 5 passed the initial gate twice and the final gate once, then took two review comments on the open PR. Neither was a correctness or fidelity defect — the test passed and the coverage was right. Both were idiom: a framework-hashed CSS class where the ladder wanted a role, and a `pmmTest.step` around a lone assertion. The checklist has entries for missing scenarios, weakened assertions, unverified locators, unselectable scenarios and practice violations, but nothing that asks whether a construct matches what the rest of `e2e_tests` actually does. Both defects were settled in under a minute once counted: `getByRole` versus `[class*=]` by the ladder, and 4-of-168 steps wrapping a lone assertion. The gates had the evidence available and no rule telling them to gather it.
- Proposed change: Add a "precedent by count" check. For each construct the migration introduces that has an alternative — locator form, step granularity, navigation idiom, POM shape, assertion style — count its occurrences across `e2e_tests` and record the ratio, then justify anything below roughly a tenth of the dominant form. Cheap, mechanical, and it produces the number that ends the argument instead of duelling prose. Note the two shapes already known to escape: a ported CodeceptJS selector that resolves but sits at the bottom of the ladder, and a step wrapping a single bare `expect`.
